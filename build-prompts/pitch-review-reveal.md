# Build the pitch review screen — "the reveal, and the door"

> Version: v0.100
> Generated by the founder-package skill (product spec flow).

Build the next version of tinker's post-publish review screen (the "arrangement"
screen) from this prompt. The founder has worked through a guided interview that
distilled the next slice into the structure below; this is the imperative form.

The feature already exists and works: when an essay is published, the screen
holds back the existing pitches behind a skeleton, then reveals where the essay
landed — the pitch it locked into rises to the top, or a "You found a new
direction" callout appears up top — with per-pitch colors, proportional bars,
and a grounded "why it didn't fit" line. This prompt takes it one step further.

## Anti-patterns — do not repeat

- **Don't turn the reveal into a dashboard.** The founder's hard line: *"never become a busy dashboard."* No stat grids, no filters, no row of competing buttons. One calm reveal moment is the whole point — adding a third and fourth action breaks it.
- **Don't frame a new direction as a failure.** The current copy ("None of your existing pitches caught this one") reads as faintly apologetic. The founder's words: *"all of the pitches of my life help sharpen my foundership and none of them are unnecessary."* A new direction is a gain, another thread — never a detour, an orphan, or a thing that "didn't fit."
- **Don't fabricate a reason or summary.** Every summary and every "why" stays grounded in real data (covered headings, the existing pitches, the verbatim phrase). tinker never invents prose for the founder; the classifier/namer only return a one-word title, so there is no model rationale to quote.
- **Don't default to a feed or a tool shape.** This is a single-moment reveal, not a list you scroll or a panel you operate.

## Read first

- `src/renderer/renderer.js` — the review screen lives here: `showWritingFit(essay)` drives the reconsidering → settling → locked lifecycle, and `renderArrangement()` builds the markup (rows, new-direction callout, slot, actions). The action buttons (`data-arrangement-action="done"` / `"read"`) are wired at the bottom of `renderArrangement`.
- `src/renderer/styles.css` — all `.arrangement__*` styles, including `.arrangement__newdir*` (the new-direction callout), `.arrangement__row*`, and `.arrangement__actions`.
- `src/renderer/pitches.js` — pitch state + API on `window.tinkerPitches`: `getPitch`, `getPitches`, `findPitchForWriting`, `coveredHeadings`, `setActivePitch`, `getActivePitch`.
- `src/renderer/pitch-script.js` — the on-camera "pitch script" view; reachable via `window.tinkerShowPitchScript(pitchId)`.
- `src/renderer/sidebar-tree.js` — the sidebar deck (the 11 slides of the active pitch).
- `README.md`, `package.json`.

## What you're building, in one paragraph

After an engineer publishes an essay, the review screen already reveals where it
landed. This version makes the reveal a **launch point**: from the single moment
of "here's the pitch this strengthened" (or "here's the new direction you
found"), give the founder one clear door **into that pitch** so they keep
building the thread that just grew, instead of bouncing back to a list. At the
same time, retune the language so every landing — especially a brand-new
direction — reads as additive to the founder's foundership.

## Phase plan

- **Single-surface build, web-first.** tinker ships one `src/renderer/` codebase across web/PWA, Electron, and Capacitor (see README). This screen is renderer-only, so building and testing it in a desktop browser (`npm run web`, or `npx vercel dev` for the authed flow) covers all three shells — no native work required.

## Build sequence

1. **Retune the landing language to be additive.** In `renderArrangement()` (and the `arrangement__newdir*` block), revise the new-direction copy so it affirms rather than apologizes. The "why" should still be grounded in real data (the existing pitches that weren't on this beat), but framed as *"this is its own thread"* — not *"none of your pitches caught this."* Keep it one or two lines; keep it true.
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Show the before/after copy for: (a) a new pitch with a slide heading, (b) an essay that couldn't be placed at all. Confirm the tone reads as a gain, not a miss, before continuing.

2. **Add one door into the pitch.** Add a single primary call-to-action on the locked reveal — *"Open this pitch →"* — that navigates into the pitch the essay landed in (the locked pitch, or the new-direction pitch). It must work for the new-direction case too (the brand-new pitch). Respect the no-dashboard line: this is **one** primary action. Consolidate the existing `Done` / `Read it →` buttons so the founder sees one clear primary door plus, at most, one quiet secondary — not three peers.
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Demo the door from both a "locked into existing pitch" reveal and a "new direction" reveal. Confirm the action set still reads as one calm choice, not a button bar. Surface the destination decision (see Open questions) for the founder before locking it in.

3. **Make sure the door survives the edge cases.** If the essay couldn't be slotted anywhere (no pitch to open), the primary door degrades gracefully — fall back to the existing read/done action rather than offering a dead "Open this pitch" that goes nowhere. No broken or empty destinations.
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Confirm the no-placement case has a sensible single action and no dangling door.

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

- **One calm reveal, never a busy dashboard.** (Founder's verbatim line.) One primary action; at most one quiet secondary. No stats grids, filters, or button rows.
- **Every landing is additive.** No copy anywhere frames a pitch or a new direction as unnecessary, orphaned, or a miss. *"None of them are unnecessary."*
- **Grounded copy only.** Every visible string is either (a) a verbatim phrase from the founder's writing already surfaced on the screen, (b) a real data value (pitch name, covered headings, slide title, X / 11), or (c) a fixed UI string from this screen's vocabulary: `You created another essay`, `You found a new direction`, `Anchored on`, `Open this pitch →`, `Read it →`, `Done`, `Slide N of 11`. Anything outside (a)/(b)/(c) is a bug.
- **Inside the existing shell.** Build entirely within `renderArrangement()` in `src/renderer/renderer.js` and the `.arrangement__*` rules in `src/renderer/styles.css`. Reuse the per-pitch color and the existing reveal lifecycle — don't re-architect the screen.
- **Not a feed, not a tool.** Single-moment reveal shape only.

## Open questions — ask the founder, don't invent

- **Where does "Open this pitch →" land?** The founder was unsure. Three real options: (1) the pitch-script view via `window.tinkerShowPitchScript(pitchId)` — a "here's your pitch" payoff; (2) `setActivePitch(pitchId)` then home, where the sidebar deck shows the 11 slides — best for *writing into* the pitch; (3) a new focused page for just that pitch (more to build). **Recommended default if forced to ship: option 2** (make it active + open the deck), because the hero moment is "keep building the pitch that just grew." Mark `[NEEDS INPUT: door destination]` in a code comment and use option 2 until the founder decides.
- **Does the primary door replace "Read it →" or sit beside it?** Within the no-dashboard line, prefer replacing/demoting so there's one clear primary. Confirm with the founder.

If you must move forward without an answer, mark `[NEEDS INPUT]` in a code comment and pick the sensible default above.

## Done when

- After publishing, the reveal shows where the essay landed (locked pitch on top, or new-direction callout on top) **and** offers one clear door into that pitch.
- The door works for the new-direction (brand-new pitch) case, and degrades gracefully when there's no placement.
- Every landing — including a brand-new direction — reads as additive; nothing frames a direction as unnecessary or a miss.
- The screen stays one calm reveal; no dashboard creep; one primary action.
- The founder's signal is met: *"I understand that all of the pitches of my life help sharpen my foundership and that none of them are unnecessary."* (Behavioral proxy to watch: after publishing, the founder opens the grown pitch unprompted instead of leaving the screen.)

Report back: a description of the reveal in both the "existing pitch" and "new
direction" cases, the door's destination, and any `[NEEDS INPUT]` decisions you
marked.
