# Build the tinker writing engine — the guided interview

> Version: v0.100
> Generated by the founder-package skill (product spec flow).
> Reconstructed from shipped code by the build-prompts audit (2026-05).

This is the heart of tinker: an onboarding-shaped guided writing flow. Claude
asks the founder **one question at a time**; the founder answers in their own
words; Claude **stitches the answers into a single essay using only the
founder's words** — it never invents prose. The flow opens when a draft tab is
selected, runs a paginated question loop with progress dots (not a chat), and
once the essay is stitched it publishes straight through — there is no separate
review/Save step — routing the founder to the post-publish "being assessed"
confirmation. The whole surface is
already shipped in `src/renderer/writing.js`; this prompt is the imperative form
of what's there. Read that file end to end before you change anything.

## Anti-patterns — do not repeat

The load-bearing rule of this engine is: **never author words for the founder,
never paraphrase — stitch only.** These come verbatim from
`writing.js`'s `SYSTEM_PROMPT`; treat them as non-negotiable.

- **Don't write prose for the founder.** *"RULE 1 — INTERVIEW, DO NOT WRITE. You ask one question at a time. You never invent prose for the founder. You never paraphrase, smooth, or improve their words. The essay is built from their typed answers, exactly as typed (you may join with paragraph breaks and trim leading/trailing whitespace, nothing else)."*
- **Don't author the stitched essay.** *"RULE 2 — STITCH, DO NOT AUTHOR. When you produce the stitched essay, every sentence must be a direct copy of words the founder has typed... You may NOT add transition phrases, summary sentences, framing language, or any words the founder has not already typed. If you find yourself wanting to add a word, do not."*
- **Don't invent a title.** *"RULE 3 — TITLE FROM THEIR WORDS. If you provide a title, it must be a contiguous phrase the founder has typed. Pick the most evocative one. Do not invent a title."*
- **Don't run a long interview, and don't drift off learning.** *"RULE 4 — KEEP IT SHORT, AND PURSUE LEARNINGS. Aim for between five and nine questions total."* Every question must contain the word `learning` or a close synonym (the rule lists: discovering, noticing, figuring out, realising, understanding, picking up, working out, coming to see, finding out, recognising), varied across questions.
- **Don't ask about feelings.** *"Do NOT ask about feelings, emotions, or moods. Do NOT ask 'how did that make you feel'. Do NOT psychoanalyse. Stay on the learning — what they are coming to understand. The founder's emotional state is not the subject."*
- **Don't break the JSON contract.** *"RULE 5 — RESPOND IN STRICT JSON."* Exactly the keys `{ "next_question", "stitched_title", "stitched_body", "done" }`. *"Never wrap the JSON in code fences. Never add explanations outside the JSON."*
- **Don't lead from the scene context.** Place, what they're facing, last purchase, and transactions set the *mood* only (RULE 6 / RULE 8): *"Do NOT assume what they are learning from any of these — never lead, never name their facing or their last purchase back to them as a fact."* The transactions are *"a mirror, not advice... Do NOT lecture about money or taxes. Do NOT moralise."*
- **Don't name the pitch back to the founder.** RULE 9 steers toward uncovered starter-pitch territory but: *"Do NOT name a slide title back to the founder. Do NOT mention the pitch, the deck, the eleven slides, or any of the slide-title literals."*
- **Ask in the founder's own writing voice, but only their voice.** RULE 10 folds in a learned writing-voice profile (`window.tinkerVoice.interviewerBlock()`, trained from the founder's own essays by `/api/voice/model`) so questions are *phrased* in the founder's cadence and vocabulary. It shapes HOW you ask, never WHAT they answer: *"It does NOT relax any rule above — every question still pursues what they are learning (RULE 4), and the stitched essay is still built only from words the founder typed (RULE 1, RULE 2). Never quote the profile back to the founder."* No trained voice yet → the canonical system prompt, unchanged.
- **Don't trust the model's stitch blindly.** The stitched body is verified token-by-token against the founder's corpus (`verifyFounderOnly`). On failure, fall back to joining the founder's raw answers by paragraph breaks. The verifier is the safety net; do not remove it.
- **Don't make it a chat.** One question per screen, large input, progress dots, `Next →` / `This is everything →` footer. No message bubbles, no streaming reply, no conversation transcript on screen.

- `src/renderer/index.html` (`<section id="writing">`, lines ~200–219) — the chrome: `#writing-stage`, `#writing-progress`, `#writing-step`, `#writing-close`, and the footer `<footer class="writing__foot">` with `#writing-end` (`This is everything →`) and `#writing-next` (`Next →`).
- `src/renderer/index.html` (`<section id="read">`, lines ~221–243) — the read view where a finished essay is read: `#read-body`, the actions menu (`#read-menu-trigger`, `#read-menu-pop`) with `Archive` / `Delete`.
- `src/renderer/renderer.js` — the wire-up. `openDraft(id)` calls `window.tinkerWriting.open(draft)` (~line 313); `newDraft` / `tinkerNewSession` create the draft; the renderer implements `window.tinkerOnDraftChange` (persist), `window.tinkerOnWritingClose`, and `window.tinkerOnWritingPublish` (~lines 1213–1215). `showRead(essay)` (~line 356) renders the read view; `store.publish` (~line 185) creates the essay and calls `showPitchAssessing(essay)`.
- `api/claude/converse.js` — the Stytch-gated proxy for the writing turns. Model `claude-opus-4-7`; wraps the system prompt in `cache_control: { type: "ephemeral" }` for prompt caching. The renderer reaches it through `window.tinker.callClaude(...)`.
- `api/search.js` — the search edge, model `claude-haiku-4-5` (the tinker browser's search engine, a sibling proxy on the same Stytch-auth pattern). Not part of the stitch loop, but the same `api/` Claude-proxy shape.
- `api/voice/model.js` + `src/renderer/voice-model.js` — the **personal writing-voice model** (RULE 10). The endpoint reads the founder's `essays` blob from `TinkerUserData`, signs the corpus, and (re)trains a structured voice profile (`voiceCard`, `tone`, `cadence`, `vocabulary`, `signatureMoves`, `avoids`, `interviewerStyle`) via `claude-opus-4-8`, caching it under `(userId, "voice-model")` (stamped with the analyser `model`); GET reuses the cache when the corpus is unchanged, retrains when it grew. A one-time `scripts/retrain-voice-models.js` backfill rolls a model upgrade across every founder's existing corpus at once. The client (`window.tinkerVoice`) caches the profile in localStorage, refreshes after `tinker:writing-saved`, and exposes `interviewerBlock()` — the fragment `writing.js` appends to the system prompt in `moodSeedQuestion` and `askNext`.
- `src/renderer/sidebar-tree.js` — exposes `window.tinkerTree.uncoveredHeadings()`, which feeds RULE 9. The eleven `DECK_HEADINGS` are: The Problem, A Persona, Why Now?, The Team, The Product, How We Make Money, Go to Market, The Moat, The Vision, Competition, The Ask.
- `build-prompts/pitch-review-reveal.md` — the surface the published essay routes into. This prompt hands off to it at publish; do not rebuild the post-publish confirmation or placement notification here.

## What you're building, in one paragraph

When a draft tab is selected, `renderer.js` calls `window.tinkerWriting.open(draft)`,
which takes over the centre column (`<section id="writing">`) while the sidebar
stays visible. A fresh draft first shows a **seed pre-prompt** ("Where have you
been and where are you going?") capturing where the founder is physically, what
they're facing, and the last thing they purchased — optional metadata that
moods the questions but never enters the transcript. Then the engine seeds the
canonical opening question `SEED_QUESTION = "What are you learning?"` (mood-tuned
by `moodSeedQuestion` via `claude-opus-4-7` when scene context exists), and runs
a one-question-at-a-time loop: the founder types an answer, presses `Next →`
(or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter), and `commitAnswer` appends the turn and calls `askNext`,
which asks Claude — through `api/claude/converse.js` (`claude-opus-4-7`) — for
either the next question or a stitched essay, returned as strict JSON. The
founder can press `This is everything →` at any point to force the stitch
(`endNow` → `askNext({ forceStitch: true })`). Every question pursues what the
founder is learning; scene context, recent transactions
(`window.tinkerTransactions.list()`), and uncovered starter-pitch territory
(`window.tinkerTree.uncoveredHeadings()`) thread into the prompt as mood and
steer, never as facts. When the stitch arrives, the body is verified
token-by-token against the founder's own words (`verifyFounderOnly`); if it
fails, the engine falls back to joining the raw answers by paragraph breaks —
provably founder-only. Once the essay is stitched it **publishes straight
through** (`doPublish` → `window.tinkerOnWritingPublish`) — no review/Save step.
The essay can later be read in the **read view** (`<section id="read">`), and
publish routes the founder to the post-publish "being assessed" confirmation
(covered by
`build-prompts/pitch-review-reveal.md`).

## Build sequence

1. **Mount the chrome and the public entry point.** Build `<section id="writing">`
   exactly as in `index.html`: a top bar with `#writing-close`, `#writing-progress`
   (dots), `#writing-step` (the step label), a `#writing-stage` body, and the
   `<footer class="writing__foot">` holding `#writing-end` (`This is everything →`,
   `disabled` by default) and `#writing-next` (`Next →`). Expose
   `window.tinkerWriting.open(draft)` as the single entry point. `renderer.js`
   calls it from `openDraft(id)` when a draft tab is selected; hold the draft in
   a module-level `active` and persist every mutation through
   `window.tinkerOnDraftChange(active.id, patch)`.

2. **Seed pre-prompt.** On a fresh draft (`active.seed === undefined`), render the
   scene card ("Where have you been and where are you going?") with three
   single-line inputs — physical place, what they're facing, last purchase —
   plus a `Skip for now` affordance and a `Continue →` button. Committing or
   skipping sets `seed` / `facing` / `lastPurchased` to a string or `null` (never
   re-prompts). This is metadata only: it does not enter the transcript and does
   not affect stitching or founder-only verification.

3. **Seed the opening question and run the question loop.** With no transcript
   yet: if any scene context exists, call `moodSeedQuestion(seed, facing,
   lastPurchased)` (model `claude-opus-4-7`, `maxTokens: 80`) to mood the
   canonical `SEED_QUESTION = "What are you learning?"` to the scene — keeping
   the learning intent and the `learning`-or-synonym rule intact — and fall back
   to the canonical seed on any failure or empty context. Render one question per
   screen (`renderPendingQuestion`): the question as an `<h2>`, a large
   `<textarea>` (placeholder *"Type your answer in your own words…"*), the recall
   chips for any scene context, and debounced `_scratch` persistence so a
   half-typed answer survives a reload. `Next →` and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter call
   `commitAnswer(question, value)`. Plain Enter inserts a newline.
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Open a fresh draft. Confirm: the seed
   pre-prompt appears once; skipping or filling it advances to the first
   question; the opening question contains `learning` or a synonym; one question
   shows at a time with progress dots; `Next →` advances; a half-typed answer
   survives a reload.

4. **The engine turn (`askNext`).** On commit, append `{ q, a }` to
   `active.transcript`, clear `active.pending`, and call `askNext`. `askNext`
   builds the user message with `buildUserMessage` — prepending scene context,
   `Recent transactions:` from `buildTransactionsContext()` (cap 25, oldest-first),
   and `buildUncoveredPitchLines()` (the uncovered `DECK_HEADINGS`, never named
   back) — then calls `window.tinker.callClaude({ system: SYSTEM_PROMPT, messages,
   model: "claude-opus-4-7", maxTokens: 2048 })`, which posts to
   `api/claude/converse.js`. Parse the strict JSON with `parseClaude` (tolerate
   accidental code fences; on parse failure treat the whole reply as the next
   question). If `done && stitched_body` (or `forceStitch`), go to stitch (step 6);
   otherwise set `active.pending = next_question` and re-render.

5. **`This is everything →` (`endNow`).** Pressing it captures any half-typed
   answer into the transcript, then calls `askNext({ forceStitch: true })`, whose
   `buildUserMessage` appends the instruction that the founder pressed *"This is
   everything"* and the model must stitch now. With an empty transcript, keep the
   question on screen (nothing to stitch).
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Answer 2–3 questions, then press
   `This is everything →`. Confirm the engine skips further questions and goes
   straight to the stitch/review. Separately, confirm a natural run (the model
   sets `done: true`) also reaches the review after 5–9 questions.

6. **Stitch + founder-only verification.** On stitch, take `stitched_body` and
   `stitched_title`. Run `verifyFounderOnly(body, transcript)`: tokenise the body
   and the founder's combined answers, and assert every body token appears in the
   founder's corpus. If the body is empty or verification fails, **fall back**:
   build the body by joining the founder's trimmed raw answers with paragraph
   breaks, and if the title isn't a verbatim phrase (`phraseAppearsIn`), derive
   it from `firstSentence(body)`. Store `active.stitched = { title, body }` and
   publish straight through.

7. **Publish straight through (`doPublish`).** There is no review/Save
   screen. The moment the essay is stitched (and on reopening a draft that
   already has `active.stitched`), call `doPublish`, which calls
   `window.tinkerOnWritingPublish(active, { title, body, author: "you" })`. The
   founder's next surface is the post-publish "being assessed" confirmation.

8. **Read view + publish handoff.** The published essay is later opened in
   `<section id="read">` via `showRead(essay)` — a `read__head` (subtitle =
   pitch/slide placement, falling back to the author label `you`), the title, and
   the body paragraphs, plus the actions menu (`Archive` / `Delete`). On publish,
   `store.publish` creates the essay (carrying `seed` through), clears the draft
   from the tree, dispatches `tinker:writing-saved`, and calls
   `showPitchAssessing(essay)` to route into the post-publish confirmation. **Do
   not build that confirmation or the placement notification here** — hand off to
   `build-prompts/pitch-review-reveal.md`.
   **CHECKPOINT — stop and report.** Stitch an essay. Confirm: the body contains
   only words the founder typed (force a verification failure with an odd answer
   and confirm the raw-answer fallback fires); the title is a verbatim phrase;
   stitching publishes straight through with no Save step; the essay opens cleanly
   in the read view; publish routes onward to the "being assessed" confirmation.

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

- **Founder-only output, always.** The published body must use only words the
  founder typed. `verifyFounderOnly` is the gate; the raw-answer paragraph join
  is the provably-safe fallback. Never ship a path where model-authored prose
  reaches the essay.
- **One model per call site.** Writing turns and the mood-seed call use
  `claude-opus-4-7` (matching `api/claude/converse.js`). The search edge
  (`api/search.js`) uses `claude-haiku-4-5`; the seed/taxonomy classifier
  (`src/renderer/heatmap.js`) and `api/classify/index.js` use
  `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`. **There is no `claude-sonnet-4-6` in this
  codebase — never write it.**
- **Strict JSON contract.** Every writing turn returns exactly
  `{ next_question, stitched_title, stitched_body, done }`, no fences, no prose
  outside the object. `parseClaude` must tolerate stray fences and fall back to
  treating the reply as a question.
- **One question at a time. Not a chat.** Large input, progress dots, paginated.
  No message bubbles, no streaming, no on-screen conversation log.
- **Five to nine questions; every question pursues learning.** Each question
  contains `learning` or a close synonym, varied. No questions about feelings,
  emotions, or moods.
- **Scene context, transactions, and uncovered pitch territory mood and steer —
  never lead.** Do not assume what the founder is learning. Do not name a slide
  title, the deck, or the eleven slides back to the founder. Do not give money,
  tax, or bookkeeping advice from the transactions.
- **Server-side key, Stytch-gated.** All Claude calls go through `api/` proxies
  that re-authenticate the Stytch session and hold the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`
  server-side; the renderer never holds the key (the only client-side key path is
  the developer `localStorage.setItem('ANTHROPIC_API_KEY', …)` escape hatch shown
  in `renderError`).
- **Persist every mutation.** Through `window.tinkerOnDraftChange`; transcript,
  pending, stitched, title, seed/facing/lastPurchased, and `_scratch` all survive
  a reload. Drafts live in `tinker.drafts.v1`, published essays in
  `tinker.essays.v1`.
- **No motion, tinker chrome only.** Instant state changes; inherit the existing
  shell. No new design language for this surface.

## Done when

- Selecting a draft tab opens the writing flow via `window.tinkerWriting.open(draft)`
  with the sidebar still visible.
- A fresh draft shows the seed pre-prompt once; skipping or filling it never
  re-prompts and never enters the transcript.
- The opening question is `What are you learning?` (or a scene-mooded variant
  that keeps the learning intent), and every subsequent question contains
  `learning` or a synonym.
- The loop shows one question per screen with progress dots; `Next →` and
  Cmd/Ctrl+Enter commit; `This is everything →` forces the stitch.
- Each writing turn calls `claude-opus-4-7` through `api/claude/converse.js` and
  returns strict JSON; no `claude-sonnet-4-6` anywhere.
- The stitched body passes `verifyFounderOnly`, or the raw-answer fallback fires;
  the title is always a verbatim founder phrase.
- Once stitched, the essay publishes straight through (`doPublish`) — there is
  no review/Save step.
- Publishing creates an essay (readable in `<section id="read">`) and routes the
  founder to the post-publish "being assessed" confirmation
  (`build-prompts/pitch-review-reveal.md`).
